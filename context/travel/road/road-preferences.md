# Road Trip Preferences

Rules for multi-day European road trips.

## Default trip profile

- 4–8 stops in a logical loop
- 1–3 nights per stop
- max ~4 hours driving per day
- group: 2 people, compact car
- origin: BER
- keep flight and drive logistics realistic

## Interest priority

When a stop has multiple interesting elements, use this order:

1. 🥾 hiking
2. 🎨 art / contemporary culture
3. 🏛️ major landmarks
4. 🌿 botanical gardens
5. 🍷 regional food / local specialties
6. 🏊 swimming

## Hard rules

- prefer direct BER flights where available
- keep same-city arrival and departure compact
- do not recommend specific restaurants by name
- mention local food culture and notable districts instead
- flag closures, seasonal issues, and booking needs with `⚠️`
- keep route timing, stops, and map labels consistent

## Practical planning

- add a few optional detours or short “Unterwegs” stops between main stops
- prefer a loop structure over backtracking
- use weather and seasonal conditions to adjust the plan
- keep the itinerary grounded in verified data, not generic filler
